Essentials

Travel Tips

Wisdom gathered from years on the road.

  • Pack only what you need — every extra kilogram is a burden on the trail.
  • Learn "hello" and "thank you" in the local language. It opens more doors than you imagine.
  • Travel insurance is not optional. Neither is a backup of your passport.
  • Wake up early. The best light, the quietest streets, the most authentic moments happen at dawn.
  • Eat where the locals eat. If the menu has photos, walk on.
