Streamix pipeline

  1. Angular `valueChanges` copies a snapshot into the `streamix` scope.
  2. `debounce(650)` compresses rapid edits into one autosave event.
  3. `derived()` computes completion, readiness, and primary skills.
  4. `scope()` owns the activity log and cleanup lifecycle.
